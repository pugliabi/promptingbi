- Expand the specific Scope toggles named in the frame column; leave the rest collapsed (the collapsed-toggle look is part of the canonical-skeleton story).
- For milestone pages, the money frame is the top: news callout + next steps side by side.
- Article pages themselves (the ✍️ drafts) are NOT demo pages — never screenshot those; they're the source of these shot lists.
