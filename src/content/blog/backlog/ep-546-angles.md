## ★ Write first leftover: Adoption Is a Threat Problem, Not a Power BI Training Problem

**Thesis:** Teaching people how to use the tool is the default adoption program. The actual blocker is whether they trust the number and whether the number can get them fired. Train later.

**Material (Tommy):**
- “We always focus on, we need to teach people how to use Power BI. But to me, that’s always missing the point.”
- Two questions the training plan never asks: do they trust what they’re looking at, and how will this affect them.
- Empathy: every person is affected by the data, so they look at it differently. Quota dashboards and email-open owners will go on guard. That’s not ignorance of slicers.
- You can be technically right (GA sampling, paused keyword, red open rate) and still lose the room, then inherit motivated skepticism on every later report.
- Nightingale’s finding, translated: the gap is not analytical skill. It’s when and why people choose to apply scrutiny, which is about identity and prior belief, not DAX literacy.

**Freshness:** leftover. Distinct from the design-pattern backlog (*report the why*) and from the named Notion psychology/design tickets. This is the adoption-program claim those pieces never made the thesis.
